ANNOTATION GUIDE -- DOMESTIC PROCEEDINGS IN ECtHR ARTICLE 6 JUDGMENTS
Guide version 3.1 (2026-11-25) -- ontology/echr.ttl 4.1.0

1. THE IDEA IN ONE PARAGRAPH
----------------------------
Read the FACTS of a judgment (never "The Law") and write down the domestic
proceedings the way the Court itself would count them in the appendix of a
committee judgment: each SET of proceedings, the act that STARTED it, and the
DECISION taken at each level. For every event you also record who was
involved, what it was about, how it ended and whether that was good or bad for
the applicant. Nothing else is an event.

    Set P1: criminal proceedings against the applicant
      start     12 Apr 1995  police question the applicant as a suspect
      decision  22 Nov 1999  District Court: convicted          (first instance)
      decision  27 Sep 2000  Higher Court: quashed, sent back   (appeal; reviews the 1999 decision)
      decision  12 Feb 2001  District Court: convicted again    (first instance; reviews the 2000 decision)
      decision   9 May 2001  Higher Court: conviction upheld    (appeal; appealed by the applicant)


2. SETS OF PROCEEDINGS
----------------------
A set is ONE claim, charge, complaint or application, followed through all its
levels until it is finally decided or is still pending.

  Same set:
    - first instance, appeal, cassation, constitutional complaint;
    - a re-examination after the case is sent back (remitted);
    - a reopening that actually leads to the case being heard again;
    - an appeal against an administrative decision, to a court, inspector or
      tribunal.

  New set:
    - a new claim in another court or forum, even about the same dispute;
    - each applicant's OWN action, even if one court decides them together;
    - a civil claim lodged inside criminal proceedings (it has its own start);
    - enforcement of a judgment (link it with `enforces`, section 6);
    - a complaint about the length of proceedings to a domestic court;
    - proceedings before a non-State tribunal (a church court, arbitration, a
      sports or professional body): record the tribunal as the body.

  Not a set at all:
    - proceedings against or by other people only (co-accused tried
      separately, witnesses);
    - private agreements with no public body; general laws and rules.

These lists are not exhaustive. If a proceeding does not clearly fit, use
your judgement and explain it in a `note`.


3. THE START ACT
----------------
The one act that opened the set. At most ONE per set. Leave it out if the text
does not narrate it with a date.

  Civil       the claim is lodged with the court.
  Administrative / requests
              the applicant's request or application to the authority
              (a visa, a permit, a benefit). If the applicant only challenges
              an administrative decision in court, the start is that court
              challenge.
  Criminal    the EARLIEST act directed at the applicant that the applicant
              knew of or was substantially affected by: arrest, being charged,
              being questioned as a suspect, notice that an investigation was
              opened against them. A complaint that leads nowhere, or a
              warrant never served, does not start anything.
  Complaints  the applicant's complaint to the prosecutor or police.

Anything lodged AFTER the set is open is not a start: appeals, the other
side's applications, counterclaims, the State's own requests.

4. DECISIONS
------------
Every ruling that DECIDES the set at one level, or sends it back:
  - a judgment, a dismissal, an appeal decision, a cassation or constitutional
    decision (a decision rejecting a complaint as inadmissible counts);
  - a prosecutor's decision to discontinue or not to prosecute;
  - an administrative decision on the applicant's own request, or one the
    applicant had to challenge first before going to court;
  - a settlement approved by, or concluded with, a public body;
  - for criminal sets, the decision that finally fixes the sentence.

The guiding question for this is - did it decide the claim, charge or request at the given level?

An inspector's, tribunal's or court's decision on an appeal against an
administrative decision `reviews` that administrative decision.

5. NOT EVENTS -- LEAVE THESE OUT
--------------------------------
  hearings, adjournments, summonses
  appeals, objections, counterclaims and other filings inside a set
      (keep who appealed and when on the decision: `appealed_by`, `appeal_date`)
  procedural orders: transfer, suspension, resumption, joinder, accepting a
      case, appointing experts, extending time limits, recusal
  rulings only on costs (record who bears the costs in `costs` on the decision)
  rulings DURING a trial that do not decide it: evidence, witnesses, no case
      to answer, detention or bail during trial, lawyers, injunctions, stays
  an appeal lodged but not yet decided (mark the set pending instead)
  investigative measures: searches, seizures, freezing property, inspections,
      questioning (except as a criminal start), expert reports
  execution steps: payments, transfers, release from prison, removals
  administrative measures nobody challenged and nobody asked for (a public
      body's own internal steps, e.g. a fund's sale decisions)

6. WHAT TO RECORD
-----------------
For every SET
  kind          Criminal | Civil | Administrative | Constitutional |
                Disciplinary | Other | Unknown
  label         a few words naming it
  pending       true if the text says it is still pending
  enforces      enforcement sets only: the decision being enforced

For every EVENT (start act or decision)
  date          as precise as the text allows: "2005-11-29", "2005-11", "2005"
  body          the court or authority, as the text names it
  authority_kind  Judicial | Prosecutorial | Administrative | Disciplinary | Unknown
  parties       everyone on a side at THIS event, with their side:
                  Initiating   moved the body to act here (claimant,
                               prosecution, appellant)
                  Responding   the one it was brought against (defendant,
                               accused, respondent on appeal)
                  ThirdParty   joined on neither side
                  Unknown
                The deciding body is never a party. Judges are never parties.
  matter        what it was about, when it is about one of these (a list is
                fine; leave it out for a purely procedural ruling):
                  Arrest         being apprehended or taken into custody
                  Detention      remand / pre-trial detention ordered, extended, ended
                  Bail           release pending trial asked for, granted, refused
                  Charge         being charged or indicted
                  Conviction     the trial or an appeal on the charge: conviction,
                                 acquittal, sentence
                  Investigation  a criminal investigation or a refusal to open one,
                                 and steps in it (a seizure in an investigation)
                  Damages        compensation claimed or awarded
                  Custody        custody of or guardianship over a PERSON: parental
                                 custody, child return, legal capacity or
                                 guardianship of an adult
                  Employment     dismissal, reinstatement, wages, work terms
                  Pension        pensions, social security, welfare benefits
                  Property       a dispute over the asset itself: ownership,
                                 restitution, tenancy, planning
                  Extradition    extradition, deportation, removal
                  LegalAid       legal aid or legal-aid counsel
                  Vetting        security clearance or similar vetting
                  Disciplinary   professional or employment discipline
                  Requests       applications about the conduct of the proceedings
                                 themselves (recusal, counsel, adjournment, access
                                 to the file)
                  Health         medical treatment or care
                  Immigration    visas, residence, asylum
                  Enforcement    enforcing a judgment
                  Other          about something specific not listed
                  Unknown        about one of these, but the text does not say which
                (Property is a dispute over the asset itself; a seizure in a
                criminal investigation is Investigation; confiscation as a
                penalty is Conviction + Property.)
  quote         the sentence that narrates it, copied exactly

For every DECISION, also
  level         Investigative | AdministrativeReview | FirstInstance | Appeal |
                Cassation | Constitutional | CaseReview | Reopening | Unknown
  outcome       MeritsDecided       the substance was decided
                Inadmissible        rejected without reaching the substance
                Remitted            quashed and sent back
                SettledOrWithdrawn  discontinued, settled, withdrawn
                Other | Unknown
  direction     how THIS decision came out for the applicant:
                FavourableToApplicant    they got what they wanted
                UnfavourableToApplicant  they did not
                Mixed     the same decision partly granted and partly refused
                          the same claim or charge
                Unknown   cannot be read
                A remittal or an inadmissibility ruling still has a direction
                when it helps or hurts the applicant: quashing a conviction is
                favourable, quashing an acquittal unfavourable, rejecting the
                applicant's appeal as inadmissible unfavourable.
                "The applicant" is the future Strasbourg applicant. Each
                decision has its own direction; never summarise the whole case.
  reviews       the earlier decision in the same set that this one rules on:
                the decision appealed against, the decision reviewed on
                cassation or by a constitutional court, the administrative
                decision a court reviewed, or the decision that sent the case
                back. Empty for the first decision of a set, and empty when
                a decision merely comes next (a trial after the
                investigation, a second step of one administrative
                procedure): the order is already given by the dates.
  appealed_by   when `reviews` is filled: who brought the appeal or
                application -- Applicant | OpposingParty | Both | Unknown
                (OpposingParty = the other litigant, the prosecution or the
                State)
  appeal_date   when the appeal or application was lodged, if the text says
  communication_date  when the decision was served on or communicated to the
                applicant, if the text says
  costs         who this decision orders to bear the costs of the proceedings:
                Applicant | OpposingParty | Both | Unknown. Leave it out
                when the text says nothing about costs. A separate ruling
                only on costs is not a decision: record its result here, on
                the decision of that level it belongs to.


7. WHEN UNSURE
--------------
  - Closed lists: write Unknown when the event exists but the text does not
    say. Never guess from what you know about the legal system.
  - Free text and dates: leave the field out when the text gives nothing.
  - Two mentions are the SAME event when they have the same body and the
    same date (name variants of one court are the same body).
  - Genuinely torn between two readings: pick one and write a `note` addressing this.


8. FILE FORMAT
--------------
One YAML file per judgment, with the fields above. The annotation app
(annotation_tool/, see its README) writes it for you; by hand, copy the layout
of annotation/test_simple/001-77778.yaml. Both are checked against these lists
by `uv run python -m art6.model FILE --text-dir TEXTS` and the test suite
(tests/test_set_model.py).
