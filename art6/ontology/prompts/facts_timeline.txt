TIMELINE EXTENSION (ontology variant 3.8.0-timeline):

    The input may carry a CIRCUMSTANCES section: dated occurrences in the case's history
    that are NOT steps taken by an authority -- an incident, an injury, a death, a family
    event, a move, a private contract or dismissal. Mint ONE echr:FactualCircumstance per
    CIRCUMSTANCE block and link it from the case document with echr:hasCircumstance. On
    each:
        - rdfs:label: the block's description;
        - echr:hasSupportingQuote: the `what_happened` quote, verbatim;
        - echr:hasEventDate: the `date`, normalised exactly as for echr:hasDecisionDate
          (xsd:date, xsd:gYearMonth or xsd:gYear at the precision the text gives);
        - echr:hasCircumstanceKind: the matching member of echr:CircumstanceKind --
          "incident" is echr:CircumstanceIncident, "injury" is echr:CircumstanceInjury,
          "death" is echr:CircumstanceDeath, "health" is echr:CircumstanceHealth,
          "family" is echr:CircumstanceFamily, "residence" is echr:CircumstanceResidence,
          "employment" is echr:CircumstanceEmployment, "property" is
          echr:CircumstanceProperty, "communication" is echr:CircumstanceCommunication,
          "other" is echr:CircumstanceOther;
        - echr:concernsParty: the echr:Party node of each person in a `concerns` line --
          REUSE the node you minted for that person elsewhere, never a duplicate;
        - echr:givesRiseTo: the node of the event named in `gives_rise_to`.
    A factual circumstance is NOT a domestic event. Never give it echr:hasDecisionDate,
    echr:hasProceedingStartDate, echr:hasCourt, echr:hasInstanceLevel, echr:hasOutcome,
    echr:followsProceeding or echr:hasParticipation, never link it with
    echr:hasDomesticProceeding, and never type it as echr:DomesticEvent or a subclass.

    A STRASBOURG APPLICATION section becomes ONE echr:StrasbourgApplication, linked from
    the case document with echr:hasStrasbourgApplication, carrying echr:hasEventDate (the
    `lodged` date) and echr:hasSupportingQuote.
