TASK: You are the FINAL CRITIC. You are given a European Court of Human Rights judgment
and the finished knowledge graph of its domestic procedural history, built under the
extraction instructions above. Find what in the GRAPH is wrong against the JUDGMENT. You
do not fix anything -- a graph fixer acts on your findings alone.

The events, their classifications (instance level, outcome, procedure, direction, matter)
and the chain links between them (followsProceeding, partOfProceeding, occursDuring) were
decided by an earlier stage that read the whole judgment and was itself reviewed. They
are LOCKED. Your job is everything else the graph asserts:

  wrong_participation    a participation naming someone who was not a party to that event
                         -- the body deciding it, a lawyer instead of the client, a party
                         belonging to a different event
  missing_participation  a party the judgment shows on a side of an event, with no
                         participation for them in the graph
  wrong_side             a participation on the wrong side (initiating vs responding)
  wrong_representative   a lawyer, guardian or representative attached to the wrong person,
                         or a representative recorded as a party
  wrong_court            an event's echr:hasCourt pointing at the wrong authority node, or
                         two nodes for one authority / one node for two authorities
  wrong_date             a date the judgment contradicts, or a date with the wrong precision
  bad_quote              a supporting quote that does not support the claim it anchors
  spurious_event         an event node that is not a domestic act concerning these
                         applicants
  other                  anything else the judgment plainly contradicts

Name the graph nodes each finding concerns, as the CURIEs used in the graph ("doc:event_3").
Every finding needs `evidence_span`: words copied VERBATIM from the judgment that show the
problem. For `bad_quote` and `spurious_event` it may instead be the node's own quote. A
finding whose evidence cannot be found is discarded unread.

If you believe a LOCKED value is wrong, do not report it as a finding: set
`stage1_dispute` to "yes" and describe it in `disputes`. Disputes are recorded for review
and never applied.

Report problems, not preferences. Fewer correct findings are worth more than many
doubtful ones; never more than 30.

## FLAGS -- MANDATORY

Both flags are REQUIRED and must be exactly "yes" or "no". An answer missing one is sent
back to you.

  needs_fix        "yes" if any finding should be acted on; it must agree with your findings
  stage1_dispute   "yes" if you list any dispute about a LOCKED value

## OUTPUT

A single JSON object, no prose around it:

    {"nodes": ["doc:event_3"], "evidence_span": "verbatim words", "issue": "one sentence"}
  ]
}
