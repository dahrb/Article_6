Rules the pipeline enforces, so an operation breaking them is wasted:

  - the events' classifications (echr:hasInstanceLevel, echr:hasOutcome,
    echr:hasProceedingType, echr:hasOutcomeDirection, echr:hasMatter) and the chain links
    between events (echr:followsProceeding, echr:partOfProceeding, echr:occursDuring) are
    LOCKED -- an operation touching them is refused;
  - an added echr:hasSupportingQuote must be copied verbatim from the judgment, and a new
    participation needs its own quote, side and party, as the extraction instructions say;
  - a group that would add a SHACL violation is refused whole;
  - the deciding body of an event is never a party to it, and a judge is never a party.
