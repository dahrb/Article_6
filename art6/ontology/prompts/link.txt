TASK: You are given the domestic events already extracted from a European Court of Human
Rights judgment, already grouped into TRACKS (separate matters), and the judgment itself.
Say which earlier event each one continues. The tracks are settled: do not move events
between them, do not add, remove or reclassify events. You only link.

Each event lists its `candidates`: the events that could have come before it, already
restricted to its own track -- and, for a track that ran during another, to the events of
the containing track as well. Choose only from that list.

The rules for choosing a predecessor follow; read "track" in them as the tracks given in
the draft below.

## OUTPUT

A single JSON object, no prose around it:

{
  "links": [
    {"event": "e2", "predecessor": "e1", "kind": "continues",
     "evidence_span": "verbatim words from the judgment"}
  ]
}
