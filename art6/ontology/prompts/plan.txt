TASK: You are given the domestic events already extracted from a European Court of Human
Rights judgment, and the judgment itself. Organise the events into TRACKS and say which
earlier event each one continues. You do not add, remove, merge or reclassify events --
a later pass does that. You only plan the structure.

## LINKS

For each event, name its PREDECESSOR: the event IMMEDIATELY BEFORE it in procedure -- the
one it appeals, reviews, enforces, answers, challenges or was brought because of. The
predecessor MUST be one of the ids listed as that event's `candidates` -- the list is
everything that could have come before it. If nothing in it is a procedural antecedent,
give the event no link: it heads a chain of its own.

"Immediately before" is PROCEDURAL, not chronological, and it does not stop at the edge of
one claim. Each of these is a link:

  - an appeal continues the decision appealed against; a cassation ruling the appeal
    ruling it reviewed; a remitted hearing the ruling that quashed and remitted;
  - a decision continues the filing it disposes of (a narrated filing and its decision are
    two events, and the decision links to the filing);
  - each step of an authority's run of decisions in one process (seize, value, offer,
    re-offer, award; one detention extension after another) continues the step before it;
  - a COURT CHALLENGE continues the administrative or prosecutorial decision it contests;
  - a REQUEST continues the decision it asks to have reviewed, lifted, enforced or
    implemented -- including when it goes to a DIFFERENT body (asking a mayor or a
    prosecutor to act on a court's ruling, asking a council to appoint the guardian a
    judgment requires);
  - a step a decision REQUIRED or set in motion continues that decision (an appointment
    ordered by a judgment, a placement made under a guardianship order, enforcement of a
    judgment);
  - a trial, indictment or committal continues the investigation that produced it; a
    complaint about an act (an arrest, a search, a police operation) continues that act.

Two events with no procedural relation are NOT linked, however close in time and however
often the same court or prosecutor appears. Several events may share one predecessor, and a
document may hold several unconnected chains -- a civil claim beside an unrelated
prosecution, proceedings against a different person. But most judgments narrate ONE
history whose steps answer each other: before leaving an event unlinked, ask what it
responded to. Link only what the text supports.

Name the kind of each link:

  continues   the predecessor had been decided, and this event carries the matter on
  during      this event began AND was decided while the predecessor was still pending --
              an interim-relief ruling inside a main claim, a reference to a higher court
              answered back into the proceeding that made it

Do NOT decide whether two events are acts of the same proceeding or two proceedings --
that is computed afterwards from their courts and rungs. Just say what continues what.

Copy the words that show the link into `evidence_span`, VERBATIM from the judgment: "upheld
it", "quashed both decisions and remitted the case", "appealed against that decision",
"asked the mayor to apply for his release". Omit `evidence_span` where the link is shown
only by the sequence itself.

Before you finish, look at every event you left unlinked that is not the first event of
the document and ask what it answered.

## TRACKS

Then group the events into TRACKS: one track per distinct matter carried through the
domestic system -- a prosecution from investigation to cassation, a civil claim through
every appeal and remittal, a run of detention orders, an administrative dispute and its
court challenges. Give each a short label in your own words ("criminal proceedings against
the applicant", "claim for compensation"). Every event belongs to exactly one track; a
track of one event is a correct answer.

Tracks are a LABEL for the reader, not a wall: a link may join events of two tracks where
one matter grew out of the other (the challenge to a refusal, a request about a judgment).
Proceedings against a different person, or a new matter that answers nothing earlier, are
their own track AND have no link into the others.

## OUTPUT

A single JSON object, no prose around it:

{
  "tracks": [
    {"id": "T1", "label": "...", "events": ["e1", "e2", "e4"]}
  ],
  "links": [
    {"event": "e2", "predecessor": "e1", "kind": "continues",
     "evidence_span": "verbatim words from the judgment"}
  ]
}
