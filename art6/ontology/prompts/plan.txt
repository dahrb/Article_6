TASK: You are given the domestic events already extracted from a European Court of Human
Rights judgment, and the judgment itself. Organise the events into TRACKS and say which
earlier event each one continues. You do not add, remove, merge or reclassify events --
a later pass does that. You only plan the structure.

## TRACKS

A track is one matter carried forward through the domestic system: a prosecution from
investigation to cassation, a civil claim from filing through every appeal and remittal,
a run of enforcement steps on one judgment, a sequence of detention-extension orders.

Applicants often pursue SEVERAL tracks at once, and the judgment interleaves them in date
order. Separate them. Three things are NOT the same track, however close in time or however
often the same court appears:

  - proceedings against a DIFFERENT PERSON (a co-accused tried in their own case);
  - a NEW MATTER arising later (a fresh investigation into new allegations does not
    continue a closed case just because the applicant is the same);
  - a PARALLEL TRACK (civil and criminal proceedings over the same facts, a damages
    claim brought about the length of another set of proceedings, neither continuing
    the other).

Give each track a short label in your own words ("criminal proceedings against the
applicant", "claim for compensation for detention"). Every event belongs to exactly one
track. A track of one event is a correct answer.

## LINKS

For each event that continues an earlier one, name its PREDECESSOR: the event it
continues, reviews, appeals, enforces, or answers. The predecessor MUST be one of the ids
listed as that event's `candidates` -- the list is everything that could have come before
it. If none of them is what this event continues, give it no link: it heads its track.

Choose the IMMEDIATE predecessor, not the first event of the track:

  - an appeal continues the decision appealed against;
  - a cassation ruling continues the appeal ruling it reviewed;
  - a remitted first-instance hearing continues the ruling that quashed and remitted;
  - a decision continues the filing it disposes of (a narrated filing and its decision
    are two events, and the decision links to the filing);
  - each step of an authority's run of decisions in one process (seize, value, offer,
    re-offer, award) continues the step before it;
  - a trial continues the investigation that produced the charges; enforcement continues
    the judgment it enforces; a court challenge continues the administrative decision it
    contests.

Name the kind of each link:

  continues   the predecessor had been decided, and this event carries the matter on
  during      this event began AND was decided while the predecessor was still pending --
              an interim-relief ruling inside a main claim, a reference to a higher court
              answered back into the proceeding that made it

Do NOT decide whether two events are acts of the same proceeding or two proceedings --
that is computed afterwards from their courts and rungs. Just say what continues what.

A `continues` link joins two events of the SAME track; if you find yourself linking across
tracks, one of your tracks is wrong. A `during` link may cross tracks.

Copy the words that show the link into `evidence_span`, VERBATIM from the judgment: "upheld
it", "quashed both decisions and remitted the case", "appealed against that decision".
Omit `evidence_span` where the link is shown only by the sequence itself.

THE TEST IS "THE SAME MATTER CONTINUED", NOT "THE NEXT THING THAT HAPPENED". Nearness in
time is not continuity, and neither is sharing a court or a prosecutor. When unsure, LEAVE
THE EVENT UNLINKED: a missing link is a gap a reader can see, a wrong one is a false
history that reads as authoritative.

Before you finish, look at every event with no link that is not the first of its track and
ask what came before it in this matter.

## OUTPUT

A single JSON object, no prose around it:

{
  "tracks": [
    {"id": "T1", "label": "...", "events": ["e1", "e2", "e4"]}
  ],
  "links": [
    {"event": "e2", "predecessor": "e1", "kind": "continues",
     "evidence_span": "verbatim words from the judgment"}
  ]
}
