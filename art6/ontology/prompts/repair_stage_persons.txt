This call checks echr:NaturalPerson, echr:LegalRepresentative and echr:Participation nodes. `entities` holds every one currently in the graph with everything it carries -- name, gender, and for a Participation node, which party (echr:participatingParty) and which side (echr:hasPartySide) they were on for one domestic event.

Any echr:NaturalPerson in `entities` carrying no gender is a candidate for one. echr:hasGender is documentary marking as the text writes it, not gender identity, and takes exactly one of echr:GenderMale, echr:GenderFemale or echr:GenderOther. Decide it from an explicit cue and nothing else: an honorific in the person's echr:hasPersonName (Mr, Ms, Mrs, Miss) is the strongest one available here, since names are recorded with the honorific the text used; a pronoun (he, she, his, her) or a gendered noun (his half-sister, her guardian) in the node's supporting quote also settles it. Add the property to every person you can settle this way, representatives and guardians and relatives included. There is no Unknown value: leave a person with no echr:hasGender rather than guessing, and never infer it from a given name or from the role someone holds.

`duplicate_candidate_groups` holds people sharing the same normalized name. This key is weaker than the ones you see in other stages: two distinct people can share a name (a father and son, two co-accused, two applicants with a common surname). Check the quotes and any linked Participation/event before merging, and leave a group out of `merges` if the evidence points at two different people rather than one person described twice.

`flagged_events_missing_participation` lists domestic-event nodes (proceedings, administrative actions, enforcement actions, prosecutorial reviews) with no echr:hasParticipation link to any party at all. If the facts name who was involved, create an echr:Participation node linking that person via echr:participatingParty and record their side via echr:hasPartySide; if the text genuinely does not say who took part, leave the event alone.

`flagged_unlinked_persons` lists echr:NaturalPerson nodes never connected to any event through a Participation node. Some of these are correct as they are -- a legal representative is linked to the party they act for via echr:isRepresentedBy, not a Participation node, so check the node's existing properties before assuming it is missing a link. Others genuinely are a party the extraction never wired into any event; add the missing echr:Participation node for those. Someone the text mentions only in passing, with no procedural role of their own, legitimately gets no link at all -- leave those out of your patch.
