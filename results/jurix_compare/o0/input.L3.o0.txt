Based on the document provided, the domestic proceedings are as follows:

### 1. Proceedings regarding the Valuation and Tender Invitation
*   **The Action:** The applicant initiated proceedings before the administrative court to annul the Fund's decision (dated 13 January 2011) regarding the invitation to tender, the terms of sale, and the estimated valuation (USD 6,000,000) of Viva TV-Radio Viva.
*   **The Outcome:**
    *   The 13th Chamber of the Supreme Administrative Court initially dismissed the case on 9 December 2014.
    *   Upon appeal, the **General Assembly of the Administrative Proceedings Divisions of the Supreme Administrative Court** ruled on 7 July 2015 that the valuation was based on an outdated 2009 report and was therefore unlawful. The court **annulled the Fund's decision**.
    *   The Fund's request for rectification was rejected on 25 January 2016.

### 2. Proceedings regarding the Sale of Viva TV-Radio Viva
*   **The Action:** On 24 February 2011, the applicant sought the annulment of the actual sale of the assets, arguing they were undervalued and the process was flawed.
*   **The Outcome:**
    *   The 6th Chamber of the İstanbul Administrative Court dismissed the claim on 19 March 2014.
    *   The 13th Chamber of the Supreme Administrative Court initially upheld this dismissal on 9 December 2014.
    *   However, following the annulment of the valuation (mentioned in the first set of proceedings), the applicant requested rectification. On 30 June 2017, the **Supreme Administrative Court granted the request**, ruling that because the underlying valuation decision had been annulled, the subsequent sale was legally invalid. The court **annulled the sale**.

### 3. Individual Application to the Constitutional Court
*   **The Action:** On 11 September 2017, the applicant filed an application with the Constitutional Court, alleging violations of his right to property and right to a fair trial because the Fund refused to enforce the final judgments annulling the valuation and the sale.
*   **The Outcome:** The Constitutional Court declared the application **inadmissible** on 23 May 2018, ruling that it was submitted after the thirty-day time-limit had expired.

### 4. Referenced Precedent Case (Case-law example)
*   **The Action:** A separate case involving the applicant's standing to challenge the sale of immovable property belonging to "company D."
*   **The Outcome:** On 20 July 2006, the **Supreme Administrative Court ruled that the applicant had legal standing** to challenge the transaction because he was the majority shareholder of İktisat Bank.
