### 1. Disciplinary Proceedings (Bar of Ukraine)
*   **QDC of Zakarpattya:** Following a complaint by another lawyer (D.), the Qualification and Disciplinary Commission (QDC) of Zakarpattya held a hearing in the applicant's absence on 17 April 2013. However, due to a change in the Rules of Procedure (requiring a majority vote for approval), the draft was rejected on 22 July 2016, and the suspension remained valid.
