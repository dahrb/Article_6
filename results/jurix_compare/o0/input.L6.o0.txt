Based on the document provided, the domestic proceedings are as follows:

**1. Commercial Court (Initial Claim)**
*   **What happened:** The applicant company sued the LLC to recover debts and loans. The LLC filed a counterclaim and later requested that the case be left unexamined because the original contracts contained an arbitration clause.
*   **Outcome:** The Commercial Court granted the LLC's request and left both the claim and counterclaim unexamined, ruling that the dispute fell under the jurisdiction of the Arbitration Tribunal.

**2. Commercial Court (Appeal)**
*   **What happened:** The applicant company appealed the decision to leave the claim unexamined, arguing that subsequent agreements had shifted dispute resolution to the courts and that the loan claim was unrelated to the arbitration clause.
*   **Outcome:** A three-judge bench of the Commercial Court rejected the complaint, finding that the claims were interconnected and stemmed from the same commercial relationship.

**3. Arbitration Tribunal of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Armenia**
*   **What happened:** Following the court's direction, the applicant company lodged a claim with the Tribunal to recover the loan and interest.
*   **Outcome:** The President of the Tribunal refused to start proceedings, stating that the specific documents the claim was based on (the promissory note and the 2005 agreement) did not contain an arbitration clause.

**4. Yerevan Civil Court**
*   **What happened:** The applicant company filed a new claim for the loan and interest, arguing that its right of access to a court had been violated since both the Commercial Court and the Arbitration Tribunal had refused to hear the case.
*   **Outcome:** The court initially accepted the claim and granted a freezing injunction on the LLC's assets, but later transferred the case to the District Court due to the abolition of the Yerevan Civil Court.

**5. Kentron and Nork-Marash District Court**
*   **What happened:** The LLC requested that the court leave the claim unexamined, citing the previous Commercial Court decision.
*   **Outcome:** The District Court left the claim unexamined, ruling that the Arbitration Tribunal's refusal to hear the case did not prove that applying to the Tribunal was impossible.

**6. Civil Court of Appeal**
*   **What happened:** The applicant company appealed the District Court's decision.
*   **Outcome:** The Court of Appeal upheld the decision to leave the claim unexamined, suggesting the applicant had failed to provide the Tribunal with the necessary contract extracts.

**7. Court of Cassation**
*   **What happened:** The applicant company lodged an appeal on points of law.
*   **Outcome:** The Court of Cassation declared the appeal inadmissible. It ruled that the Arbitration Tribunal had refused the case because the applicant failed to submit an original or certified copy of the arbitration agreement, and therefore the applicant was not deprived of judicial protection.
