Based on the document provided, the domestic proceedings are as follows:

### 1. Criminal Proceedings against the Applicant (Malkhaz Baduashvili)
*   **Investigation:** Following the murder of V.T. on July 10, 2005, the applicant was arrested and remanded in custody on July 27, 2005. The investigation relied on statements from witnesses M.T. and Z.T., as well as the victim's family. During this phase, the investigator rejected M.T.'s request to correct his statements and rejected the applicant's requests to confront M.T.
*   **First-Instance Trial:** On December 8, 2006, the court convicted the applicant of aggravated murder and the illegal acquisition, possession, and carrying of firearms, sentencing him to 17 years’ imprisonment. The court dismissed the applicant's argument that witness statements were obtained under duress.
*   **Appeal:** On June 27, 2007, the Tbilisi Court of Appeal upheld the conviction, finding no evidence that pressure had been applied to the witnesses.
*   **Supreme Court:** On February 20, 2008, the Supreme Court declared the applicant's appeal on points of law inadmissible.

### 2. Criminal Proceedings against Witnesses (M.T. and Z.T.)
*   **Trial:** Separate proceedings were opened against the witnesses for obstructing justice by giving false testimony (after they changed their testimony during the applicant's trial). On December 26, 2006 (M.T.) and January 23, 2007 (Z.T.), both were convicted at first instance and given partially suspended sentences.

### 3. Investigation into Alleged Ill-Treatment
*   **Investigation:** Following the applicant's complaint to the Chief Prosecutor in August 2010—alleging he had been beaten by the governor of Rustavi Prison no. 6 and pressured to withdraw his application to the European Court of Human Rights—an investigation was opened on August 31, 2010.
*   **Outcome:** The investigation included forensic medical examinations and questioning of prison staff and doctors. The forensic report found only minor abrasions, and the prison governor denied the allegations. The document does not state the final legal outcome of this investigation.
