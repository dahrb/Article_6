Based on the document provided, the domestic legal proceedings in Slovenia involving Mr. Matko are as follows:

### 1. Criminal Complaint filed by the Applicant (against police officers)
*   **The Complaint:** On April 7, 1995, Mr. Matko made an oral complaint, followed by a written criminal complaint on May 15, 1995, against unidentified police officers for unlawful deprivation of liberty and causing minor bodily harm.
*   **The Investigation:** The Public Prosecutor requested the police to identify the officers involved. The Ministry of Internal Affairs (MIA) formed a "working group" which concluded that all police procedures were lawful and that the officers acted within their powers.
*   **The Outcome:** On January 17, 1997, the Public Prosecutor dismissed the complaint, concluding there was no reasonable suspicion that the officers had committed any criminal offenses. Mr. Matko did not pursue the option to initiate prosecution as a subsidiary prosecutor.

### 2. Criminal Proceedings against the Applicant
*   **Initial Charges:** On April 12, 1995, the police lodged a complaint against Mr. Matko for "obstructing an official in the course of his duties."
*   **Investigation and First-Instance Decision:** In May 1997, the Slovenj Gradec District Court initially decided **not** to open a criminal investigation, citing contradictions in the police reports and the failure to identify the officers allegedly attacked.
*   **Appeal and Reversal:** The Public Prosecutor appealed, and on December 4, 1997, the Maribor Higher Court overturned the lower court's decision and ordered the criminal investigation to proceed.
*   **First Trial:** Following an indictment in December 1998, the Slovenj Gradec District Court **acquitted** Mr. Matko on November 22, 1999, ruling that it had not been proven that he physically resisted the officers.
*   **Retrial:** The Public Prosecutor appealed the acquittal. The Maribor Higher Court quashed the judgment and ordered a retrial. On February 12, 2001, the Slovenj Gradec District Court **convicted** Mr. Matko, sentencing him to three months' imprisonment (suspended for three years). The court relied on the testimony of police officers and dismissed the testimony of the applicant's witnesses.
*   **Final Appeal:** Both the applicant and the prosecutor appealed. On May 9, 2001, the Maribor Higher Court upheld the conviction. The proceedings were officially concluded on October 11, 2001, following a final order regarding court costs.
