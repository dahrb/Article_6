Based on the document provided, the domestic proceedings are as follows:

**1. Proceedings against the European Space Agency (ESA)**
*   **The Action:** In October and November 1993, Mr. Beer and Mr. Regan instituted proceedings before the **Darmstadt Labour Court** (*Arbeitsgericht*). They argued that under the German Provision of Labour (Temporary Staff) Act, they had acquired the status of employees of ESA.
*   **The Outcome:** On 21 March 1995, the Darmstadt Labour Court declared the actions **inadmissible**. The court ruled that ESA validly relied on its immunity from jurisdiction under the ESA Convention and the German Courts Act. The court rejected the applicants' argument that ESA was bound by a previous agreement (concerning ESOC) that had subjected its predecessor organization to German jurisdiction.

**2. Proceedings regarding Mr. Regan's dismissal**
*   **The Action:** Mr. Regan instituted proceedings in the **Darmstadt Labour Court** against his dismissal by his employer, the Italian company T.I.
*   **The Outcome:** A **settlement** was reached on 6 September 1994. It was agreed that his employment terminated on 31 December 1993, and T.I. agreed to pay him 22,000 marks (DEM) for loss of job.

**3. Proceedings regarding Mr. Beer's dismissal**
*   **The Action:** While not described as a court proceeding in the same detail as the others, the document notes a legal dispute regarding Mr. Beer's dismissal by the French company T.
*   **The Outcome:** An **agreement** was concluded on 20 June 1995 terminating his employment. The company T. agreed to pay him 14,000 DEM for lawyer's fees incurred in labour court proceedings concerning his dismissal and 305,000 DEM for the loss of his job.
