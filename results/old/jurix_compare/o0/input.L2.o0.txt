Based on the document provided, the domestic proceedings are as follows:

### 1. Proceedings against the European Space Agency (ESA)
*   **The Forum:** Darmstadt Labour Court (*Arbeitsgericht*).
*   **The Action:** In October and November 1993, Mr. Beer and Mr. Regan sued ESA, arguing that under the German Provision of Labour (Temporary Staff) Act, they had acquired the status of ESA employees.
*   **The Outcome:** On 21 March 1995, the Labour Court declared the actions **inadmissible**. The court ruled that ESA enjoyed immunity from jurisdiction under the ESA Convention and its Annex I, as well as section 20(2) of the German Courts Act. The court rejected the applicants' argument that ESA was bound by a previous agreement (concerning the former ESRO) that had subjected its predecessor to German jurisdiction.

### 2. Proceedings regarding dismissal (Mr. Philip Regan)
*   **The Forum:** Darmstadt Labour Court (*Arbeitsgericht*).
*   **The Action:** Mr. Regan instituted proceedings against his employer, the Italian company T.I., following his dismissal.
*   **The Outcome:** A **settlement** was reached on 6 September 1994. Mr. Regan's employment with T.I. terminated on 31 December 1993, and the company agreed to pay him 22,000 marks (DEM) for loss of job.

### 3. Employment termination agreement (Mr. Karlheinz Beer)
*   **The Action:** While not a court proceeding in the same sense as the others, the document notes a legal agreement concluded on 20 June 1995 between Mr. Beer and the French company T.
*   **The Outcome:** The contract was terminated on 30 June 1995. The company agreed to pay Mr. Beer 14,000 DEM for lawyer's fees incurred in labour court proceedings regarding his dismissal and 305,000 DEM for the loss of his job.
