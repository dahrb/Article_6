Based on the document provided, there were two distinct sets of domestic proceedings: those initiated by the applicant against the police, and those initiated by the state against the applicant.

### 1. Proceedings initiated by the applicant (Criminal Complaint)
The applicant filed complaints alleging that he had been beaten, threatened, and electro-prodded by police officers during his arrest.

*   **The Complaint:** On 7 April 1995, the applicant made an oral complaint, followed by a written criminal complaint on 15 May 1995, against unidentified officers for causing minor bodily harm and unlawful deprivation of liberty.
*   **The Investigation:** The Public Prosecutor requested the police to identify the officers involved. The Ministry of Internal Affairs (MIA) and the Slovenj Gradec Police submitted reports stating that all procedures were lawful and that the applicant had resisted arrest.
*   **The Outcome:** On 17 January 1997, the Public Prosecutor dismissed the complaint, concluding that the officers had acted within their duties and powers. The applicant did not pursue the matter as a subsidiary prosecutor.

### 2. Proceedings initiated by the state (Criminal Prosecution of the Applicant)
The state prosecuted the applicant for "obstructing an official in the course of his duties."

*   **Initial Phase:** The police lodged a complaint in April 1995. In May 1997, the Slovenj Gradec District Court initially decided **not** to open a criminal investigation due to contradictions in the police reports and the failure to identify the officers allegedly attacked.
*   **Appeal and Investigation:** The Public Prosecutor appealed, and the Maribor Higher Court overturned the decision, ordering the investigation to proceed.
*   **First Trial:** The Slovenj Gradec District Court **acquitted** the applicant on 22 November 1999, finding that the prosecution had not proven he physically resisted the officers.
*   **Retrial:** The Public Prosecutor appealed the acquittal. The Maribor Higher Court quashed the judgment and ordered a retrial. On 12 February 2001, the District Court **convicted** the applicant, sentencing him to 3 months' imprisonment (suspended for 3 years). The court relied on the testimony of police officers and dismissed the testimony of the applicant's witnesses.
*   **Final Outcome:** Both the prosecutor and the applicant appealed the conviction. On 9 May 2001, the Maribor Higher Court **upheld the conviction**. The proceedings were finally concluded on 11 October 2001.
