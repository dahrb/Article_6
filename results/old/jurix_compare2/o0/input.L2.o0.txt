Based on the document provided, the domestic legal proceedings in Germany were as follows:

**1. Proceedings against the European Space Agency (ESA)**
*   **The Action:** In October and November 1993, Mr. Beer and Mr. Regan instituted proceedings before the Darmstadt Labour Court (*Arbeitsgericht*). They argued that under the German Provision of Labour (Temporary Staff) Act, they had acquired the status of employees of ESA.
*   **The Defense:** ESA claimed immunity from jurisdiction based on the ESA Convention and its Annex I.
*   **The Outcome:** On 21 March 1995, the Darmstadt Labour Court declared the actions inadmissible. The court ruled that ESA enjoyed immunity from jurisdiction under the German Courts Act and the ESA Convention, rejecting the applicants' argument that ESA was bound by a previous agreement (concerning ESOC) that had subjected its predecessor organization to German jurisdiction.

**2. Proceedings regarding dismissal (Mr. Philip Regan)**
*   **The Action:** Mr. Regan instituted proceedings in the Darmstadt Labour Court against his employer, the Italian company T.I., following his dismissal.
*   **The Outcome:** A settlement was reached on 6 September 1994. Mr. Regan's employment terminated on 31 December 1993, and T.I. agreed to pay him 22,000 DEM for loss of job.

**3. Employment termination agreement (Mr. Karlheinz Beer)**
*   **The Action:** While not a court proceeding, the document notes a legal agreement concluded on 20 June 1995 between Mr. Beer and his employer, the French company T.
*   **The Outcome:** The agreement terminated his employment on 30 June 1995, with the company agreeing to pay him 14,000 DEM for lawyer's fees related to labour court proceedings and 305,000 DEM for loss of job.
