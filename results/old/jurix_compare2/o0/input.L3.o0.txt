Based on the document provided, the domestic legal proceedings in Türkiye involving the applicant are as follows:

### 1. Proceedings Regarding the Valuation and Tender Invitation
The applicant challenged the Savings Deposit Insurance Fund's (the Fund) decision of 13 January 2011, which set the estimated value of "Viva TV-Radio Viva" at USD 6,000,000 and approved the tender terms.
*   **First Instance:** The 13th Chamber of the Supreme Administrative Court dismissed the case on 9 December 2014.
*   **Appeal:** On 7 July 2015, the General Assembly of the Administrative Proceedings Divisions of the Supreme Administrative Court **annulled the Fund's decision**, ruling that the valuation was based on an outdated 2009 report and did not reflect current market value.
*   **Outcome:** The Fund requested rectification on 25 January 2016, but the Supreme Administrative Court rejected it, making the annulment final.

### 2. Proceedings Regarding the Sale of Viva TV-Radio Viva
The applicant sought the annulment of the actual sale of the assets to "Company A."
*   **First Instance:** On 19 March 2014, the 6th Chamber of the İstanbul Administrative Court dismissed the claim, finding no irregularities in the sale.
*   **Appeal:** On 9 December 2014, the 13th Chamber of the Supreme Administrative Court upheld the dismissal (though two judges dissented).
*   **Rectification:** On 30 June 2017, the 13th Chamber of the Supreme Administrative Court granted the applicant's request for rectification. Citing the previous annulment of the valuation (from the first set of proceedings), the court ruled that all actions based on that invalid valuation were legally invalid.
*   **Outcome:** The court overturned the lower court's decision and **annulled the sale**.

### 3. Enforcement Requests (Administrative)
Following the court victories, the applicant twice requested that the Fund enforce the annulment judgments (in March 2016 and August 2017).
*   **Outcome:** The Fund refused both requests, claiming that enforcement was legally and practically impossible because the assets had already been transferred to a "bona fide" third party and the proceeds had been spent.

### 4. Individual Application to the Constitutional Court
The applicant filed an application with the Constitutional Court on 11 September 2017, alleging violations of his right to property and right to a fair trial due to the Fund's failure to enforce the judgments.
*   **Outcome:** On 23 May 2018, the Constitutional Court declared the application **inadmissible** because it was submitted after the 30-day legal time limit had expired.
