Based on the document provided, the domestic legal proceedings in Armenia are as follows:

**1. Commercial Court (2007)**
*   **The Claim:** The applicant company sued A. Safaryan and Associates LLC to recover debts and loans. The LLC filed a counterclaim to annul debt agreements, alleging fraud.
*   **The Outcome:** On 16 November 2007, the Commercial Court left both the claim and counterclaim unexamined. It ruled that because the original contracts contained an arbitration clause, the dispute fell under the jurisdiction of the Arbitration Tribunal of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Armenia.
*   **Appeal:** The applicant appealed this decision to a three-judge bench of the Commercial Court, but the complaint was rejected on 10 December 2007.

**2. Arbitration Tribunal of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Armenia (2008)**
*   **The Claim:** Following the Commercial Court's ruling, the applicant lodged a claim with the Tribunal to recover a loan and interest.
*   **The Outcome:** On 7 October 2008, the President of the Tribunal refused to start proceedings. The Tribunal ruled that the specific documents the claim was based on (a promissory note and a 2005 agreement) did not contain arbitration clauses, and no separate agreement to use the Tribunal had been concluded.

**3. Yerevan Civil Court / District Court (2008–2009)**
*   **The Claim:** The applicant filed a new claim in the Yerevan Civil Court, arguing that since the Arbitration Tribunal had refused the case, their right of access to a court was being violated.
*   **Transfer:** The case was transferred to the Kentron and Nork-Marash District Court due to the abolition of the Yerevan Civil Court.
*   **The Outcome:** On 16 June 2009, the District Court left the claim unexamined. It held that the Tribunal's refusal letter did not prove that applying to arbitration was impossible and upheld the previous Commercial Court finding that the matter belonged in arbitration.

**4. Civil Court of Appeal (2009)**
*   **The Proceeding:** The applicant appealed the District Court's decision.
*   **The Outcome:** On 21 July 2009, the Court of Appeal upheld the decision to leave the claim unexamined, stating that the applicant had failed to provide the Arbitration Tribunal with the necessary contract extracts containing the arbitration clause.

**5. Court of Cassation (2009)**
*   **The Proceeding:** The applicant lodged an appeal on points of law.
*   **The Outcome:** On 23 September 2009, the Court of Cassation declared the appeal inadmissible. It ruled that the Arbitration Tribunal's refusal to hear the case was due to the applicant's failure to submit an original or certified copy of the arbitration agreement; therefore, the applicant had not been deprived of judicial protection.
